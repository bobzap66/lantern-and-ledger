August Shepard is a samsaran cleric of Alseta whose faith centers on passage, transition, and the meaning carried from one life into the next. Fragmentary memories of previous incarnations give him an unusual perspective on death, but they also make his present life precious: August knows that this incarnation is not disposable, and his fear of spiritual failure has followed him through every descent into the Abomination Vaults.

Within the Roseguard he became healer, moral conscience, translator, and frequently the person willing to stand in the worst possible place if it meant someone else could survive. He argued for redemption when his companions preferred expedience, sponsored refugees, helped troubled spirits find peace, and repeatedly tried diplomacy against creatures that had already begun attacking him. Those ideals were tested severely by the compromises required to stop Belcorra, and August learned that remaining loyal to imperfect people could be harder than simply declaring a principle and walking away.

His greatest enemy was often his own sense of responsibility. August catalogued failures obsessively, blamed himself for deaths he could not reasonably have prevented, and took personal mistakes particularly hard. Nhimbaloth’s psychic trials finally forced him to recognize that despair itself was being harvested. His answer was not to forget his failures, but to refuse to let something else own them. He urged his companions to face forward rather than surrender their pain to the Empty Death.

During the final descent August freed trapped souls from ancient devices, fought creatures with whatever tools proved effective, and kept the party alive through the battles leading to Belcorra’s destruction. When the Gauntlight fell, he emerged with his faith intact but less rigid than when he entered: still committed to protecting souls and opening doors, but far more aware that not every passage is clean and not every right choice arrives without a cost.

Personality

August is compassionate, intensely protective, philosophical, idealistic, and prone to guilt. He believes deeply in free will and the possibility of redemption, but experience has taught him that principles sometimes have to survive contact with terrible circumstances rather than remain untouched by them. His deepest fear is not death itself, but outliving everyone he loves and being left with no one remaining to protect.

Notable Moments

  • Defied the soul-devouring servants of Nhimbaloth despite openly admitting his fear of them.
  • Repeatedly risked himself to reach injured companions in hazards ranging from deep water to caustic slime.
  • Freed souls trapped for centuries in the machinery beneath the Gauntlight.
  • Refused Nhimbaloth’s offer to take away his failures and warned the rest of the party not to let the Empty Death feed on their despair.
  • Helped sustain the party through the final confrontation that ended Belcorra Haruvex and brought down the Gauntlight.
