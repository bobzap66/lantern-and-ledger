Tubthumpers

Adventurers, rulers, and founders of the Republic of Thumping Waters

The Tubthumpers are the adventuring company whose exploration of the Stolen Lands ultimately led to the founding of the Republic of Thumping Waters. What began as a chartered expedition into a dangerous frontier became the nucleus of a new government, and the group gradually transformed from a band of explorers into the rulers, defenders, and most visible public figures of the country they created.

The name survived every change in membership. The Tubthumpers continued to travel together, investigate threats, negotiate with neighboring powers, and personally confront dangers that could have been delegated to soldiers or officials. In practice, the line between the adventuring company and the government of Thumping Waters has often been very thin.

Current Company

The present company includes:

  • Seamus, bard and Minister of Intelligence
  • Matteo Auditore, rogue and Minister of Justice
  • Lurking Hunter, fungus leshy druid
  • Elowyn Vask, exemplar and priest of Razmir
  • Antson “Ant” Crazyfoot, ranger and Minister of the Interior
  • Ally Grainger, arcane spellcaster and Prime Minister of Thumping Waters

The current roster reflects how much the company has changed since its first expedition. Some founders retired or left, while later companions joined a group whose identity had already become inseparable from the history of the Republic.

Former Companions

Several figures remain central to the history of the Tubthumpers even though they no longer travel with the current company.

  • Mairi Blackwood was one of the founding members and a defining front-line presence during much of the Republic’s early history. She has since retired from the company.
  • Fitzroy Fulgrim III served as a champion and Minister of Defense before leaving both the company and his government role.
  • Velka joined the company later as a warpriest of Hanspur and eventually retired from active adventuring.

Their departures did not erase their place in the story. The Tubthumpers are best understood as a continuing institution rather than a fixed six-person party.

Founding Thumping Waters

The Tubthumpers first entered the Stolen Lands as adventurers rather than statesmen. Their early work brought them into conflict with the Stag Lord and the dangers of the Greenbelt, but defeating those threats created an opportunity far larger than the expedition that had begun them. The company became the founding leadership of a new realm, eventually establishing the Republic of Thumping Waters with Thumpington as its capital.

That transition did not end their adventuring career. Instead, the stakes changed. Threats that once endangered a handful of travelers increasingly endangered farms, towns, allies, and citizens who now depended on the Tubthumpers’ decisions. The company’s expeditions became inseparable from the survival and growth of the Republic.

Defenders of the Republic

Over the years, the Tubthumpers have confronted threats ranging from bandits and monsters to curses, rival rulers, ancient undead powers, and political crises. Their campaigns carried them through the destruction of the Stag Lord’s organization, the mysteries of Candlemere, the Bloom, the troll war under Hargulka, the disappearance of Varnhold and the defeat of Vordakai, the conflicts surrounding Drelev and the Tiger Lords, the Rushlight Tournament, and the war with Pitax.

Their victories did more than expand the map. Each crisis changed the country they returned to. New settlements joined the Republic, old enemies became subjects or allies, political offices changed hands, and the Tubthumpers increasingly had to answer for the consequences of decisions made both in council chambers and in the field.

Adventurers and Government

The Tubthumpers are unusual because their identity as an adventuring company never cleanly separated from their identity as national leadership. Several members hold or have held seats on the Ruling Council, and their personal relationships, rivalries, loyalties, and mistakes have repeatedly had consequences for the state as a whole.

That arrangement has made Thumping Waters unusually responsive when existential threats appear and unusually vulnerable when the people responsible for governing it are somewhere underground fighting monsters. The Republic’s history is therefore difficult to tell without also telling the story of the Tubthumpers themselves.

Legacy

The Tubthumpers began as people sent into a frontier and became the people responsible for deciding what that frontier would become. Their legacy is written into the borders, settlements, institutions, alliances, and grudges of Thumping Waters. Whether history ultimately remembers them primarily as adventurers, founders, politicians, or something stranger will depend on what survives the rest of their reign.
