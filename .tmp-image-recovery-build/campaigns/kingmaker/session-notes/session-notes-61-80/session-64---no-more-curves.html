Session 64: No More Curves

Ally completes the Helix Theorem as the passages beneath Candlemere straighten. Reconstruction from the expedition’s account.

Republic Chronicle — Restricted

Compiled for the council by Linzi, Minister of Communication. Gozran 24, 4716 AR. Not for public dissemination.

The Candlemere operation has ended. Minister of Justice Matteo Auditore and Cinder were recovered alive, Minister of the Interior Antson “Ant” Crazyfoot was restored to life, and the surviving Thresholder presence was eliminated. The ancient mechanism beneath the island has been altered so that its connection to Yog-Sothoth is no longer active. Candlemere’s oppressive atmosphere and the gathering of wisps around the island ceased after the ritual.

Matteo Auditore

Auditore was found bound to a worktable and subjected to the same intrusive memories previously inflicted by Thresholder attacks. Upon waking, he said that he had lived a thousand lives beneath Candlemere and that each had involved greater suffering than the last. The period of physical captivity was less than a day. The experienced duration cannot be measured and should not be dismissed merely because ordinary time did not pass with it.

Auditore spent three months recovering and reconstructing his professional training around alchemy. He has returned to duty, but observation should continue for delayed effects arising from the imposed memories. Questions about which recollections belong to Auditore, which belong to other lives, and whether any were deliberately selected remain unresolved.

Auditore also contributed a substantial portion of his wealth to the cathedral after the party returned. Records should distinguish that contribution from the treasure sold to obtain the materials required for Crazyfoot’s restoration.

Resurrection of Minister Crazyfoot

The expedition returned to Thumpington with Crazyfoot’s body and sold recovered treasure to obtain the rare materials required for Raise Dead. Tristian prepared the rite overnight at the Temple of the First Light. Crazyfoot returned weakened but coherent. A mark resembling a dancing flame appeared upon his arm after the rite.

After the later recovery of Cinder, Skybolt awakened a further property. Crazyfoot can now rise and remain in the air while wielding the bow. The change appears permanent and should be recorded as a property of the relic rather than a result of resurrection until evidence establishes otherwise.

Cinder and the Quelaunts

Cinder was recovered from a nightmare state in a chamber guarded by three Quelaunts. Following treatment, he reacted not only to familiar scent but to the emotions of those approaching him. Subsequent examination indicates that he can identify emotional states by scent and produce a retaliatory emotional effect when threatened.

This change may be useful, but it is not harmless. Grainger’s lingering despair caused Cinder to recoil before recognizing her. Companions should announce their approach, avoid assuming that familiarity overrides what Cinder now senses, and report any further changes in his behavior or abilities.

The Helix Theorem

Brass tablets recovered from the lecture hall completed the account begun in the scriptorium. The inverse spiral beneath Candlemere drew the attention of Yog-Sothoth through a symbolic relationship among its chambers. Closing it required a miniature gate to be carried through a ninefold sequence from the center outward.

Prime Minister Ally Grainger completed the sequence on Abadius 23. The floor moved, previously curved passages straightened into a single visible line, and the pressure long associated with the island ended. This result supports the conclusion that the complex itself formed part of the active mechanism. The ritual should not be repeated, reversed, or reconstructed elsewhere without council authorization.

Thul Vexir and the Leng Rubies

The expedition first encountered the Leng envoy identifying himself as Thul Vexir while the freezing hazard was active. A surviving Thresholder disciple attacked the party in the trapped chamber while Vexir watched with apparent curiosity and made no move to aid either side. Elowyn caught him within an area of divine attack, after which he retaliated and concentrated his attacks upon her until the disciple was killed. He then ceased fighting and returned to observing the party’s unsuccessful attempts to silence the hazard.

Vexir stated that he had been negotiating with the Thresholders about helping them bring Yog-Sothoth into Golarion. His conduct did not suggest loyalty to their cause. He appeared to be assessing them and their opponents with equal detachment, and showed no moral objection to either the proposed result or the deaths occurring in front of him. After Elowyn apologized for striking him, the two exchanged gifts. Vexir then correctly pronounced the Aklo prayers and ended the cold.

Vexir distributed valuable rubies during the exchange. Every member of the expedition accepted one except Lurking Hunter, who declined on the grounds that they had no need of rubies. Research completed during the following months established that willingly accepting one of these stones permits denizens of Leng to direct hostile mental magic against the recipient. A ruby given away returns to the person who accepted it.

Assessment: Vexir should not be classified as an ally of the Republic or an enemy of the Thresholders. His behavior is more consistent with an observer willing to assist, attack, negotiate or withdraw according to immediate interest, without a moral commitment to preventing Yog-Sothoth’s arrival. The stones are not ordinary cursed property. Their danger depends upon acceptance, appears to preserve a relationship between giver and recipient, and resists transfer. Do not sell, gift, discard, destroy, or experimentally exchange them without a controlled plan. No further gifts from Vexir or his associates should be accepted.

Numerian Frontier

On Gozran 23, the first survey toward a possible trade route crossed into Numeria and encountered two constructs identifying movement as prohibited. Attempts at translation and explanation did not prevent an attack. The constructs, provisionally identified as Radiant Wardens, used aerial movement, force projections, and concentrated attacks against spellcasters before being destroyed.

Assessment: One engagement does not establish who placed the Wardens, what territory they defended, or whether their warning applies to a larger controlled region. Future survey parties should expect additional technological defenses and should not describe the route as open, safe, or commercially useful until reconnaissance establishes all three.

Immediate priorities:

  1. Secure and study the Leng rubies without permitting additional willing recipients.

  2. Continue medical and magical observation of Auditore, Crazyfoot, Cinder, and Skybolt.

  3. Preserve the complete Helix record while restricting instructions sufficient to reproduce the gate mechanism.

  4. Survey the Numerian approaches before committing labor, funds, or merchants to a road project.

  5. Resume investigation of Ilthuliak, Minognos-Ushad, Hill Stomper, and the other unresolved western threats after the expedition has fully recovered.

Private note: Three editions ago, I asked the printers to leave space for a later announcement that everyone came home. I did not specify what condition they would be in, whether death would have to be negotiated with first, or how much of Candlemere they would carry back with them. The sentence is nevertheless true, and I intend to print it.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication

THE LANTERN AND LEDGER

News, Notices, and Practical Information for the Thumping Waters Republic

Gozran 24, 4716 AR · Published in Thumpington · Linzi, Editor · Mara Venn, Managing Editor · Price 1 cp

This account has been prepared from interviews with the returned Candlemere expedition, records of the resurrection performed at the Temple of the First Light, and reports submitted during the subsequent survey into Numeria. Details whose publication could recreate the mechanism beneath Candlemere have been withheld.

EVERYONE CAME HOME FROM CANDLEMERE

Antson Crazyfoot restored to life; Matteo Auditore and Cinder recovered; ancient danger beneath island brought to an end

By Mara Venn, Managing Editor

THUMPINGTON — Every member of the Republic’s Candlemere expedition has returned home, including one who returned by way of death.

Minister of Justice Matteo Auditore and his animal companion, Cinder, were recovered alive from the chambers beneath the island. Minister of the Interior Antson “Ant” Crazyfoot, whose death the Lantern and Ledger reported in its Abadius 22 edition, was restored through a rite performed by Tristian at the Temple of the First Light. The remaining expedition members survived the final descent, and the magical influence that made Candlemere a place of persistent dread has ended.

The result closes an operation that began with the deliberate opening of an ancient Pharasmian seal and continued through six days of fighting, retreat, rescue and investigation beneath the ruined tower. The council has not declared the island open to ordinary travel. Structural examination and a complete inventory of the lower chambers remain unfinished, but officials no longer consider an active hostile force to control the complex.

THIS EDITION AT A GLANCE

Missing companions: Matteo Auditore and Cinder recovered alive

Minister Crazyfoot: Restored to life at the Temple of the First Light

Candlemere: Ancient underground mechanism disabled; oppressive atmosphere ended

Recovery: Three months devoted to healing, study and retraining

Numerian frontier: Proposed trade-route survey encounters and destroys two hostile constructs

Public access: Candlemere remains closed pending structural and magical inspection

A RESCUE THAT REQUIRED TWO RETURNS

By Mara Venn, Managing Editor

The party found Auditore in a work chamber connected to a spherical room filled with constellations. He had been bound to a table and subjected to intrusive memories that made the hours of his captivity feel substantially longer. When Ally Grainger asked what had been done to him, Auditore answered, “I’ve lived a thousand lives down here, and every one of them suffering greater than the last.”

Auditore was conscious, injured and able to travel. Cinder remained missing. The expedition nevertheless withdrew with Auditore and Crazyfoot’s body rather than risk another death before reaching the animal’s location. Grainger used Umbral Journey for the entire return to Thumping Waters. Because the Netherworld still reflected the lake’s geography, the company first had to use the cutter to cross from Candlemere to the shadowed shore before continuing the journey. Recovered treasure was sold to pay for the rare materials required to restore Crazyfoot, and Auditore separately contributed a substantial portion of his wealth to the cathedral.

Tristian prepared the rite overnight. At the Temple of the First Light, prayer and golden light restored Crazyfoot to life, although not immediately to health. The magic left him weakened for a period of recovery and marked one arm with the shape of a moving flame. His reunion with Grainger was private except for those required to assist with the rite.

Lurking Hunter later offered the least complicated summary available to this newspaper: “I’m glad everyone who died is alive.” The Ledger has reviewed the grammar and found no useful correction.

Antson Crazyfoot is restored to life at the Temple of the First Light. Artist’s reconstruction based on temple records and participant testimony.

THE HALLWAYS’ LAST ARGUMENT

By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent

Returning below Candlemere did not mean that the way remained clear merely because its principal defenders had been killed. One corridor sealed behind the expedition while five softball-sized orbs set into its walls fired bolts of force. Seamus pressed the bell of his Horn of Blasting over one emitter in an attempt to block it and protect Auditore. The next discharge tore the horn apart. His assessment of the loss was immediate: “I really liked that thing. Seven hundred gold pieces.”

Seamus sacrifices his Horn of Blasting to block one of the corridor’s force emitters. Artist’s reconstruction from the expedition’s account.

The next hazard was a room covered in shifting Aklo prayers. Each failed attempt to pronounce the words released another wave of supernatural cold, freezing the chamber and sometimes the people within it. Grainger made progress in understanding the sequence, only to lose portions of that progress when the pattern changed. The party used healing magic, potions and Elowyn’s horn to remain alive through repeated failures.

Thul Vexir was already in the chamber. The Leng envoy watched with apparent curiosity as a surviving Thresholder disciple attacked the expedition, intervening only after one of Elowyn’s attacks also struck him. Vexir fought back and directed his attention principally toward Elowyn until the disciple was killed. He then stopped attacking and resumed watching while the trapped party struggled with the prayers and the recurring cold.

Vexir said he had been negotiating with the Thresholders over assistance with a proposal whose full nature the council has not released. Expedition members described him as appearing to judge the cultists rather than serve them, although nothing in his conduct suggested that moral objection would have prevented an agreement. He watched the party’s repeated failures until Elowyn apologized for striking him. After the two exchanged gifts, Vexir pronounced the prayers correctly and ended the cold.

His assessment of the preceding attempts was concise: “I do not wish to interfere in whatever this ritual is you have going on. You appear to be engaged in self-flagellation.” The council has not endorsed his motives, his methods or the general proposition that accepting presents from visitors from Leng is wise.

CINDER WAKES, AND SKYBOLT ANSWERS

By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent

Grainger’s divination led the expedition down to a chamber where Cinder lay unconscious and whimpering through a nightmare from which ordinary noise could not wake him. Three Quelaunts guarded the room. These floating creatures feed upon negative emotion and attack emotion as readily as flesh, overwhelming their victims with despair, fear, fascination and uncontrolled laughter while remaining beyond the easy reach of ordinary weapons and difficult to affect with emotional magic.

Elowyn protected nearby companions against the worst of the assault while Lurking Hunter changed form to reach the airborne creatures. Seamus altered his weapon to strike above him, Grainger cast lightning through the chamber, and Auditore joined Crazyfoot in keeping pressure upon enemies that would not come down willingly. Seamus killed the last Quelaunt with a single decisive blow.

Cinder woke after treatment, but he did not respond to Grainger as he had before his captivity. He recoiled from the despair still clinging to her and approached Crazyfoot only after the ranger’s arrival changed what he sensed. The precise nature of Cinder’s condition remains under examination, although his ordinary bond with Crazyfoot appears intact.

That reunion produced a second change. As Crazyfoot approached with Skybolt in hand, his feet left the floor. The bow has awakened the ability to carry its wielder through the air, an improvement that Crazyfoot accepted with considerably less hesitation than the Temple’s instructions concerning post-resurrection rest.

Cinder recognizes Ant as Skybolt lifts the ranger from the floor. Artist’s reconstruction from the expedition’s account.

CANDLEMERE LOSES ITS CURVES

By Mara Venn, Managing Editor

The final answer beneath Candlemere was not another enemy. It was a route.

Brass tablets recovered from a lecture hall completed instructions first identified earlier in the expedition. The underground complex had been arranged as a spiral whose rooms, passages and repeated symbols formed a magical relationship with something beyond the island. Breaking that relationship required the company to carry a hand-sized model gate through a precise sequence, symbolically closing the larger structure from its center outward.

Grainger performed the sequence on Abadius 23. When she reached its end, the floor moved and passages that had resisted ordinary geometry drew themselves into one straight line. Outside, the pressure long associated with Candlemere faded. The wisps gathering around the island dispersed, and observers on the shore reported that the tower no longer produced the same instinctive dread.

Scholars advising the council caution that an ended magical effect does not make an ancient underground complex structurally safe. The island therefore remains closed while the government examines what the movement damaged, opened or exposed. The distinction between “no longer bending space toward an outer power” and “suitable for an afternoon picnic” remains substantial.

THREE MONTHS TO MEND

By Linzi

The expedition did not return from Candlemere and immediately resume its ordinary business, because there was no ordinary version of what had happened to them. Crazyfoot required time to recover from death. Auditore had returned with more memories than one life could hold. Cinder understood the people around him differently, and every survivor had spent days making decisions in rooms that punished delay, speed, curiosity and occasionally pronunciation.

The council therefore allowed three months for recovery, retraining and work that did not require anyone to enter another sealed ruin. Auditore rebuilt his methods around alchemy, moving from occasional preparations to the disciplined daily practice of an alchemist. Crazyfoot recovered his strength and refined how he tracks more than one quarry. Grainger added Shape Stone, Truespeech and Phantasmagoria to her studies. Lurking Hunter improved the enchanted wrappings used in his changing forms, while the rest of the company repaired equipment, conducted research and attempted to determine what Candlemere had left with them.

They also learned that some gifts are harder to return than gratitude would suggest. Everyone except Lurking Hunter accepted one of the Leng envoy’s rubies; Lurking Hunter said simply that they had no need of rubies. The council has issued no public finding concerning the stones received beneath the island, and the Ledger will not publish instructions for testing them. Citizens are nevertheless reminded that valuable gifts from unfamiliar extraplanar envoys deserve examination before acceptance, not afterward.

PROPOSED NUMERIAN ROAD MEETS ARMED RESISTANCE

By Sella Copperclaw, Trade and Civic Affairs Correspondent

On Gozran 23, three months after the Candlemere operation, the ministers crossed into Numeria while surveying a possible western trade route. The proposal is preliminary. No road has been approved, no construction funds have been appropriated, and no merchant should yet interpret a line on a planning map as a route upon which a wagon can survive.

The case for exploration is straightforward. A reliable Numerian connection could broaden access to imported materials, create another market for goods produced in Thumping Waters and reduce dependence upon longer routes controlled by less predictable neighbors. The terrain, security and political authority along the proposed course remain considerably less straightforward.

The first survey encountered two unfamiliar constructs that advanced while ordering the party not to move. Translation established what the machines were saying but did not establish a peaceful means of obeying them, particularly after they began attacking. The constructs flew, projected concentrated force and responded aggressively to powerful spellcasting. Grainger was badly injured before her companions destroyed both machines.

Auditore identified weaknesses in the Wardens’ construction and later restored Grainger with a powerful alchemical preparation. The survey continued into the next unexplored area, but the encounter has already changed the practical calculation. If similar defenses occupy the route ahead, the cost of a road will include more than graders, bridges, guards and agreements with settlements. It will first require someone to determine whose border the machines believed they were enforcing.

The Ministry of Trade has not published a projected cost or timetable. At this stage, that is evidence of restraint rather than delay.

The survey party fights two Radiant Wardens along the proposed Numerian route. Artist’s reconstruction from the expedition’s report.

PUBLIC NOTICE: CANDLEMERE REMAINS CLOSED

The magical pressure surrounding Candlemere has ended, but the island, tower and underground complex remain closed to unauthorized travel while structural and magical inspections continue.

The closure applies to sightseeing, salvage, private research, relic hunting, devotional visits, fishing from the immediate shore and attempts to confirm that the passages are now straight by entering them without a government escort.

Previous permissions have not automatically resumed. Boat owners should continue reporting attempted private charters to the nearest watch office.

FROM THE EDITOR’S DESK

By Linzi, Editor and Minister of Communication

In our Abadius 20 edition, I wrote that there would be time to judge the opening of Candlemere when everyone came home. I asked the printers to leave room in the record for that possibility even after the expedition lost contact, retreated, returned, and began sending reports in which the word alive required increasingly careful qualification.

Everyone came home.

That sentence is true because Tristian spent a night preparing to ask Sarenrae for a life already lost. It is true because the others returned beneath Candlemere after recovering one missing companion and refused to abandon the second. It is true because they accepted that rescue might require retreat, that courage might mean leaving a room before every question had been answered, and that the work after survival can take longer than the danger itself.

Home did not restore them to the people who left. Auditore remembers lives he did not live. Cinder can smell grief. Crazyfoot bears fire upon his arm and rises when he draws his bow. Even Candlemere returned in another shape, its curved halls pulled straight and its long dread lifted from the water.

Records prefer clean endings because a page must stop somewhere. People are under no such obligation. The island’s story has ended only in the useful sense that it is no longer trying to open a gate beneath us, and the expedition’s recovery has ended only in the administrative sense that they have already gone west looking for another road.

For today, the shorter sentence is enough. Everyone came home, and the lamps in their windows are lit.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
