Session 47: Faithful’s End and Expansion

Side

:

From Linzi’s Journal

An account of events during the spring of 4714 AR, compiled from official records and the testimony of those present

There are days when the work of government concerns roads, taxes, and whether a wall ought to be built before or after someone discovers what has been eating the livestock. There are other days when a friend dies, an angel grows wings, and an intelligent sword complains that the deceased handled the experience inefficiently.

This account contains both sorts.

The Last Faithful

The battle beneath the oak resumed with Ally already suffering beneath the First Faithful’s curse. The prophet’s theology may have been incoherent, but his magic was unfortunately quite sound.

Velka was killed outright. There was no warning, no final speech, and no graceful moment in which everyone understood what had happened. One instant she stood with Ovinrbaane in her hands. The next, she was dead.

The First Faithful did not enjoy a similarly memorable end. The Tubthumpers killed him shortly afterward and dismantled the last resistance within the sanctuary. The Kingdom of the Cleansed had promised deliverance from death and decay. Its prophet died surrounded by the victims of that promise.

Among his possessions was a powerful necromantic staff. It was sold almost immediately to pay for Velka’s return. Moral debates about the proper disposition of dangerous relics become admirably brief when someone you care about is dead and resurrection has a price.

The Kobold Who Returned

Tristian performed the ritual himself. Velka returned to life bearing a scimitar-shaped mark upon her arm, which he identified as a sign of Sarenrae.

Ovinrbaane welcomed its wielder back by mocking her for Dying without making proper use of it. The sword called the incident wasted potential. Velka has continued to carry the ancient greatsword, and from this point forward it has almost entirely replaced the shield and trident with which she once fought.

Tristian’s own part in the miracle produced another revelation. Golden wings manifested from his back—briefly, brilliantly, and with remarkably little preparation for those watching. He concluded that he was angelic in nature and suggested this might explain why he had always been able to see in darkness.

I have added “unexpected angel” to my list of ministerial complications.

A Kingdom Changes Shape

With the cult destroyed, the Tubthumpers returned to the more familiar dangers of government. Fort Drelev formally pledged fealty to Thumping Waters. New walls rose, the republic’s borders expanded, and unrest diminished as the western settlements began the slow work of recovering from Hannis Drelev’s rule.

Diplomacy with the Tiger Lords fared less well.

Amiri had taken control of the tribe and meant to use it to establish a homeland for the surviving Six Bears. Once she had stood beside the Tubthumpers as a trusted ally. Now she rejected every overture from Thumping Waters and declared that this was not the kingdom she had once believed in.

The envoys returned without an agreement and with the unhappy knowledge that an old friend now ruled a neighboring people who wanted nothing from us.

Elsewhere, reports continued to arrive of missing people and livestock. Captain Valerie posted a bounty of two thousand gold for a predator known chiefly by its twenty-two-inch fangs and its impressive commitment to remaining unseen. The affected country had acquired the name Haunted Fen, which at least has the virtue of honesty.

The Spider Between Worlds

Exploration carried the Tubthumpers into the Tors of Levenies, where they met an ether spider named Zzamaas. He was intelligent, courteous, and considerably easier to negotiate with than many humanoids I could name.

Zzamaas asked the party to reclaim his hunting grounds from a band of xill occupying the country around a ghost stone. The creatures were four-armed conquerors from beyond the Material Plane, armed with bows and blades. Their method of reproduction involved paralyzing victims, implanting eggs within them, and allowing the young to burrow free later.

The Tubthumpers considered this information and killed every xill they found.

In gratitude, Zzamaas retrieved a chest hidden upon the Ethereal Plane. It contained arcane spells, unfamiliar rituals, and a wand capable of shrinking objects. More importantly, he confirmed that the surrounding region is a thin place between worlds. Creatures cross from the Ethereal Plane during the night, producing the apparitions reported by the local centaurs.

The land is haunted. It is simply haunted in a technically precise manner.

The Dragon Without a Hoard

Further exploration revealed a great cave containing the skeletal remains of a silver dragon. The wounds and evidence surrounding the corpse suggested that a black dragon had killed it.

There was no sign of the victorious dragon. There was also no sign of the silver dragon’s hoard.

Those two absences feel related.

Lake Hooktongue

The expedition ended at Lake Hooktongue, where the legendary lake monster emerged as advertised: enormous, hostile, and offended by the party’s continued existence. The confrontation involved poison, snapping jaws, spells, and the usual disagreement over which creatures are entitled to remain at the top of a local food chain.

The Tubthumpers killed it.

Velka and Seamus searched beneath the water afterward and discovered a sunken barge. Within it lay a waterproof chest containing coins, moonlit chainmail, a lifting belt, a wand of cleansing magic, and several other rewards for people willing to investigate the bottom of a monster-infested lake.

The kingdom is larger now and quieter in places that were once loud. It is also missing people, animals, treasure, and answers—and acquiring neighbors, planar breaches, and ancient swords it did not ask for.

Velka has died and returned. Tristian has wings. Amiri has a nation of her own. Somewhere, a black dragon has decided not to be found.

I will continue to write all of this down. Someone should.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
