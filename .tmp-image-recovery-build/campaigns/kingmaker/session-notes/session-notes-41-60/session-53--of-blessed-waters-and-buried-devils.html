RESTRICTED INTELLIGENCE REPORT

For council circulation only — Not for publication

Compiled by the Ministry of Communication

Zozraael and the Surviving Cult

The entity called Old Sharptooth was Zozraael, a horned devil. His manifested body was destroyed beneath Sootscale Valley. Investigators should not assume that every cult member, infernal compact, hidden shrine, or source of diabolic influence was eliminated with him.

All three kobold warpriests survived. Two surrendered after Zozraael fell; the third was captured while unconscious. They should be questioned separately regarding recruitment, other adherents, funding, sacrifices, infernal agreements, and any compromised officials within Sootscale Valley.

The prisoners’ survival provides an opportunity to distinguish committed diabolists from kobolds drawn into the cult through grievance, fear, coercion, or inherited belief. Velka should be consulted before sentences or rehabilitation plans are finalized.

Conditions Behind Recruitment

Tartuccio’s earlier abuse of the Old Sharptooth faith established a durable pattern: ritual fear, condemnation of dissenters, promises of protection from invented curses, and violence presented as necessary for communal survival.

Integration into Thumping Waters improved Sootscale Valley’s security and prosperity but did not resolve every resentment concerning autonomy, leadership, cultural change, or Mikmek’s government. Zozraael appears to have exploited those tensions rather than creating them.

A security response alone may drive remaining believers further underground. Recommend pairing investigation with public hearings, trusted kobold clergy, material support, and local authority visibly independent of Thumpington’s direct command.

Ovinrbaane Transferred

Velka relinquished Ovinrbaane when she chose to remain in Sootscale Valley. Fitzroy accepted custody of the ancient two-handed greatsword after a ritual intended to begin breaking its bond with Velka.

Ovinrbaane is an intelligent, blood-hungry artifact associated with Armag and capable of reshaping its bearer’s identity. Fitzroy’s possession must be monitored closely. The transfer should not be reported publicly.

Military Expansion

The republic recruited the Thumpington Rangers, Tail Swipers, Safety Third cavalry, and Longtail Legionnaires. Existing formations received additional magical weapons and armor where supply and training allowed.

The public edition identifies the new formations but omits their strength, equipment allocations, readiness, and deployment. Those details should remain restricted throughout the war with Pitax.

Outstanding White Rose Matters

Evindra remains within the cistern beneath White Rose Abbey. Recovery of her stolen shawl and Briar remains necessary before she is expected to provide her full knowledge of the weapon and its history.

The Gardener was dispersed but not permanently destroyed. His return remains likely unless Evindra is released from the area or the ghost is otherwise laid to rest.

Recommended Actions

Interrogate the three warpriests separately. Search for additional cult cells and infernal contracts. Support Velka’s work in Sootscale Valley without making her appear to be Thumpington’s imposed governor. Monitor Fitzroy and Ovinrbaane. Keep army deployments restricted. Recover Evindra’s shawl and Briar. Permanently resolve the Gardener’s haunting.

OLD SHARPTOOTH SLAIN BENEATH Sootscale Valley

A childhood terror proves terribly real—and finds Velka waiting for him.

By Sella Copperclaw, Civic Reporter • Historical reporting by Linzi

The creature worshipped for generations as Old Sharptooth is dead. The Tubthumpers tracked the fiend into the sewers beneath Sootscale Valley and destroyed him after a battle that repeatedly brought several members of the company to the edge of death.

Old Sharptooth was no winged kobold ancestor, as many Sootscales were once taught. He was Zozraael, a horned devil. Accounts from the surviving investigators and Sootscale witnesses indicate that Zozraael learned a cult had grown around his image and entered the valley to cultivate it for himself.

Three kobold warpriests who fought beside Zozraael survived the battle and are now in custody. Two surrendered after their patron fell; the third was captured unconscious. Officials have begun the more difficult work of determining how the cult gained followers and whether others remain hidden in the valley.

FOUR NEW ARMIES ANSWER THE CALL

By Mara Venn, Managing Editor

The republic has recruited four new formations as the War of the River Kings continues: the Thumpington Rangers, the Tail Swipers, the Longtail Legionnaires, and the cavalry company whose officers have formally—and apparently without coercion—registered the name Safety Third.

Existing forces also received additional magical arms and armor. Military officials declined to publish a complete inventory, which is reassuring evidence that someone in government remembers King Irovetti can read.

New formations muster beneath the banners of Thumping Waters. Illustration prepared from muster records and field sketches supplied to the Ledger.

Three additional territories near Lake Hooktongue were brought under the republic’s administration. Food gathering produced uneven results, but current stores remain sufficient.

In Thumpington, construction of a new hospital was completed with extraordinary efficiency and required only half the lumber originally set aside. The unused timber remains public property despite several convincing private theories regarding porches.

FROM FALSE IDOL TO LIVING DEVIL

By Linzi, Editor and Minister of Communication

The story of Old Sharptooth began long before Zozraael entered Sootscale Valley. This account draws on my own records from the first expedition into the caverns and on later conversations with Chief Sootscale, who lived under Tartuk’s rule and led the revolt that ended it. Generations of kobolds revered a small ivory figure as a mighty winged kobold. They feared his curses, offered him worship, and preserved his image without knowing it depicted a horned devil.

That fear became a weapon when the gnome Tartuccio entered the caverns disguised as a purple kobold called Tartuk. He did not create the old faith. He found it waiting for him.

Tartuk declared himself the tribe’s spiritual leader. Illusions, theatrical gestures, strange components, and a bubbling cauldron supplied the appearance of divine power. Kobolds who questioned him became “bad kobolds” and were sacrificed so that Old Sharptooth would not curse the tribe.

When mitflits from The Old Sycamore repeatedly stole the idol, Tartuk called the war a sacred test. He turned every loss into proof that the Sootscales needed him and every frightened objection into another reason to obey.

Artist’s reconstruction of Tartuk’s rule, based on Linzi’s expedition records and Chief Sootscale’s account.

Chief Sootscale finally broke that power by breaking the idol itself. He shattered the statuette and led his people against Tartuk. When the false priest died, his body returned to that of Tartuccio, revealing that the purple kobold had never existed.

The idol was broken and the deceiver exposed, but neither act could erase the fear he had cultivated. Nor could they erase every grievance born as Sootscale Valley changed from an isolated tribe into part of a larger republic.

Surviving testimony indicates that Zozraael eventually learned worshippers were invoking his image. He entered the valley not to correct their error, but to make the lie true, finding old fear, remembered sacrifice, and discontent ready to be shaped into a genuine diabolic cult.

VICTORY AT A COST

By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent

Fitzroy and Antson Crazyfoot spent two days following signs of the cult through the sewers beneath Sootscale Valley. The trail ended in cramped tunnels where Zozraael had gathered three kobold warpriests and promised a return to the old ways.

According to the company’s account, the warpriests and their patron answered the intrusion with repeated bursts of divine force that tore through the confined chamber. Ally and Seamus were both knocked senseless more than once. Velka kept Seamus alive through wounds that might otherwise have killed him, while Ally burned closed one of her own injuries and continued fighting.

Fitzroy anchored the company behind shield and spell. Ally conjured a whirling blade of force that cut through the cult’s defenses, and Seamus struck Zozraael at close range when an opening appeared. The devil fell soon after; two warpriests surrendered, while a third, already unconscious, was taken prisoner.

“Your false god is dead. Repent.” —Velka

The command was not elaborate. After generations of elaborate lies, perhaps it did not need to be.

Velka REMAINS IN SOOTSCALE VALLEY

By Sella Copperclaw, Civic Reporter

After the prisoners were secured, Velka announced that she would remain in Sootscale Valley rather than continue traveling with the Tubthumpers.

The cult had not grown in a foreign ruin or an enemy capital. It had grown among her own people, feeding upon old wounds that victory over Zozraael alone could not mend. Velka concluded that Hanspur’s work now called her home.

Velka’s departure from the adventuring company, reconstructed from accounts of the Tubthumpers and Sootscale witnesses.

She leaves active adventuring after helping transform the Sootscales from a frightened tribe ruled by false priests into citizens capable of choosing their own future. That future has proved neither simple nor safe, but it remains theirs to choose.

The Tubthumpers departed without one of their longest-serving companions. Velka remained in Sootscale Valley, where the work of identifying surviving cult members and rebuilding trust among the Sootscales had only begun.

FROM THE EDITOR’S DESK

By Linzi, Editor and Minister of Communication

Tartuccio taught the Sootscales that fear was faith. Zozraael arrived years later and offered to prove him right.

Neither deceiver succeeded alone. Each relied upon wounds already present: fear of a curse, fear of change, anger at being unheard, and the comforting promise that suffering becomes meaningful when an authority names the proper enemy.

Zozraael is dead. The three kobolds who followed him still live, and what happens to them will say something important about what Thumping Waters believes victory is for.

Velka has chosen to remain where that question is hardest. I will miss her on the road. I am glad she is staying.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
