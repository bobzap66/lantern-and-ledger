Compiled by Linzi, Minister of Communication. Not for public dissemination.

Battle East of Fort Drelev: Preliminary Military Assessment

Republican forces committed: Nok-Nok’s Irregulars, Thumpington Rangers, Lizardfolk Defenders, Safety Third Cavalry, and Noman Scouts.

Outcome: All five formations were defeated. The Noman Scouts survived as an organized force after their withdrawal. The other four formations were destroyed as effective armies, although scattered individual survivors remain possible and are already returning.

Command casualty: Governor Nok-Nok was killed while remaining with the collapsing Irregular line to protect soldiers attempting to escape.

Enemy forces: Four Tusker Rider formations remained operational at the end of the army engagement. Kob Moleg was subsequently killed by the Tubthumpers. The effect of his death upon the giants’ cohesion and loyalty has not yet been confirmed.

Enemy Dispositions

Reconnaissance placed the Pitax Horde in a forested position near the republic’s frontier. A charmed bird confirmed the presence of a large body of two-legged troops concealed among the trees.

The wyvern forces previously associated with the destruction of Littletown appear to remain in or near that settlement.

The Tubthumpers intend to approach the Pitax Horde with Kob Moleg’s head as proof of his death. The objective is to determine whether any of the seven constituent tribes can be induced to abandon Irovetti, remain neutral, or turn against Pitax.

Vision Elowyn Vask

Elowyn Vask possesses genuine divine spellcasting ability and performed effectively during the fight with Kob Moleg. Her healing was instrumental in keeping Fitzroy alive.

Her recent presence in Pitax and her affiliation with the church of Razmir warrant continued caution. No evidence presently indicates that she is acting against the republic, but her arrival immediately after Velka’s departure was unusually convenient.

Recommendations

Recover and reorganize survivors before committing further armies. Determine whether the leaderless Tusker Riders remain cohesive. Establish contact with the Pitax Horde before Irovetti can replace Kob Moleg or conceal the circumstances of his death. Maintain discreet observation of Elowyn without interfering with her work.

Editor’s private note: Suspicion is useful when it keeps one’s eyes open. It becomes useless when it prevents one from accepting the hand currently keeping Fitzroy’s blood inside him.

News, Notices, and the Public Record of Thumping Waters

Lamashan 7, 4714 AR • Published in Thumpington • Linzi, Editor • Mara Venn, Managing Editor • Price 1 cp

FOUR ARMIES LOST; Nok-Nok FALLS EAST OF Fort Drelev

Mammoth cavalry drives the republic from the field before the Tubthumpers strike down hill-giant chieftain Kob Moleg.

By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent • Additional reporting by Mara Venn and Linzi

The Republic of Thumping Waters has suffered its gravest military loss of the war. Five Republican armies engaged four formations of Pitaxian mammoth cavalry east of Fort Drelev. Every Republican formation was defeated, four were destroyed as effective fighting forces, and Governor Nok-Nok of Greengripe fell beside his Irregulars.

The Noman Scouts alone survived as an organized formation. The Thumpington Rangers, Lizardfolk Defenders, Safety Third Cavalry, and Nok-Nok’s Irregulars were shattered. Individual Survivors continue to return from the battlefield, and officials caution that a final accounting of the dead and missing will take time.

The defeat did not end without an answer. As the surviving soldiers withdrew, the Tubthumpers charged the hill from which Kob Moleg commanded the Tusker Riders. After a brutal fight, Fitzroy struck down the giant chieftain with Ovinrbaane. The company returned with Moleg’s head as proof that the architect of the mammoth host will command no further battles.

Mammoth-mounted Tusker Riders break the Republican lines east of Fort Drelev in an artist’s reconstruction.

THE MAMMOTHS BROKE THE LINE

By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent

The battle began as an effort to destroy Kob Moleg’s Tusker Riders before they could strike another settlement. Five armies marched under the banners of Thumping Waters: the Thumpington Rangers, Lizardfolk Defenders, Safety Third Cavalry, Noman Scouts, and Nok-Nok’s Irregulars.

They faced four enemy formations composed of seasoned giants mounted upon mammoths. The Republican advance initially met with some success, but the character of the battle changed once the mammoths reached the lines. Their charges scattered troops, broke formations, and left soldiers unable to recover before the next assault struck.

One Republican army fell, then another. Officers attempted to preserve an orderly withdrawal, but the field became a confusion of wounded soldiers, riderless mounts, broken equipment, and mammoths driving through every opening.

The Noman Scouts were defeated but escaped destruction and have begun regrouping. The other four formations no longer exist as effective armies. This does not mean that every soldier serving in them was killed. Survivors are still being located, treated, and returned to their homes. It does mean that the republic must rebuild much of the force it sent into battle.

The Ministry of Defense has begun compiling lists of the dead, wounded, and missing. Families awaiting news are asked to register with their local authorities so that information can be delivered as soon as it is confirmed.

NOK-NOK’S LAST STAND

By Linzi, Editor and Minister of Communication

Nok-Nok’s Irregulars held among the most dangerous positions on the field. When their formation collapsed, Nok-Nok refused to abandon the soldiers who had followed him from Greengripe.

Witnesses report that he remained behind, fighting to protect his people and buying time for any who could still escape. It would have surprised no one had Nok-Nok died attempting something reckless, spectacular, and unnecessarily explosive. Instead, he died defending his soldiers.

Governor Nok-Nok stands with the remaining Irregulars as their position is overrun.

The full account of Nok-Nok’s life appears in a separate memorial published with this edition. He was the Hero of the Mite Warrens, governor of Greengripe, commander of the Irregulars, and one of the republic’s earliest friends. He spent much of his life insisting he was a hero. In the end, he removed every possible doubt.

I questioned Governor Nok-Nok’s methods often. I never questioned his courage. He understood that soldiers do not fight for formations, regulations, or lines drawn upon a map. They fight for the people standing beside them and the homes waiting behind them. His Irregulars followed him because they knew he would never ask them to face a danger he would not face himself. In the end, he proved them right.

— High Marshal Valerie

Greengripe will honor its fallen governor with fireworks. For one night, no one is expected to enforce the rule against explosions before breakfast.

THE CHIEFTAIN FALLS

By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent

Even as the Republican army withdrew, the Tubthumpers rode toward the hill where Kob Moleg directed the Tusker Riders. If the battle could not be won upon the field, they intended to take its commander from the enemy.

Seamus opened the attack by placing destructive magic upon Antson Crazyfoot’s weapon. Ally took to the air and struck Moleg with a Chromatic Ray of extraordinary force. Elowyn Vask, newly joined to the company, followed on winged sandals and used her magic to keep Fitzroy standing through the giant’s retaliation.

That aid proved essential. Moleg struck Fitzroy twice with blows powerful enough to end most fights immediately. Fitzroy endured them, answered with Ovinrbaane, and finally brought the chieftain down with a Blessed Counterstrike.

The Tubthumpers recovered Moleg’s head, armor, immense spiked club, and other arms from the hilltop. His head will serve as proof when the republic makes contact with the Pitax Horde, whose tribes may have less reason to remain loyal now that the giant chieftain is dead.

Kob Moleg’s death does not restore the four armies lost below his hill. It does ensure that the man responsible will never lead his mammoths against Thumping Waters again.

A VISION IN A SILVER MASK

By Sella Copperclaw, Civic Reporter

Before the expedition departed, Thumpington received an unusual visitor. Vision Elowyn Vask, a priest of Razmir, addressed a gathered crowd while wearing the silver mask and blue robes of her faith.

Vision Elowyn Vask addresses citizens of Thumpington before departing with the Tubthumpers.

Elowyn did not demand immediate belief. She urged her listeners to watch, question, and remember her words when events began to change. Ally Grainger approached the priest afterward and made clear that the republic’s welcome would not require the surrender of its skepticism.

You don’t need to believe me. You don’t need to decide anything today. Just pay attention, and when things start to change, don’t ignore it—because they will.

— Vision Elowyn Vask

Elowyn recently traveled through Pitax and offered knowledge of the kingdom’s preparations for war. More importantly, she demonstrated that her healing magic is genuine. With Velka remaining in Sootscale Valley, Elowyn accepted an invitation to accompany the Tubthumpers.

Her first expedition ended with Fitzroy alive despite Kob Moleg’s best efforts. Citizens may decide for themselves whether this proves anything about Razmir. It proves something about Elowyn.

THE REPUBLIC AT WORK

By Mara Venn, Managing Editor

The council’s latest turn of government produced new roads and several public improvements. Murkwillow Hollow, a sacred grove, was established in Tok-Nikrat. Apple Boots Bar and Tavern opened in Sootscale Valley. Fort Drelev and Tatzlford received sewer systems, Willowfen gained a watchtower, and an orphanage was completed in Thumpington.

Food production proved considerably less successful. The government was forced to purchase or redirect supplies to cover the shortage. Officials remind citizens that roads, taverns, watchtowers, and civic enthusiasm are not edible, even when sufficiently seasoned.

PALETTE PRESENTED

By Tobin Bramblefoot, Games and Society Correspondent

High Marshal Valerie has presented the Tubthumpers with the Palette of Master Strokes, a remarkable magical artist’s palette capable of producing far more than flattering portraits.

The precise limits of the palette remain under examination. Citizens are asked not to submit requests for portraits, improved views from their windows, or paintings of municipal officials being chased by geese.

FROM THE EDITOR’S DESK

By Linzi, Editor and Minister of Communication

We lost a battle east of Fort Drelev. No headline can make that sentence gentler, and no account of Kob Moleg’s death should be used to hide it.

Four armies were destroyed. The Noman Scouts survived because soldiers held together after defeat and found their way home. Many others survived because someone beside them refused to run first. Nok-Nok died because he was one of those people.

There will be time to rebuild formations, replace equipment, revise plans, and decide how to face the leaderless Tusker Riders. Today belongs to the dead, the wounded, the missing, and the families waiting for a knock upon the door.

Kob Moleg is dead. Nok-Nok is dead. These truths do not balance one another upon any honest scale.

But the republic Nok-Nok helped build still stands, and tomorrow it will begin the work he left us.

Tonight, look toward Greengripe.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
