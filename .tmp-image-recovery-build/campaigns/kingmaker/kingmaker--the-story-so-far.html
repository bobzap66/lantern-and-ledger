Kingmaker: The Story So Far

The Story So Far

The adventurers who would become known as the Tubthumpers entered the Stolen Lands with a charter to explore the Greenbelt and suppress its bandits. They defended Oleg’s Trading Post, dismantled the region’s bandit network, and eventually defeated the Stag Lord. What began as an expedition became something larger when they accepted the harder task of governing the territory they had helped pacify.

Building the Republic

The Republic of Thumping Waters was founded in the aftermath, with Thumpington as its capital. The new rulers surveyed wilderness, founded settlements, built roads and public works, negotiated with neighboring peoples, and discovered that a nation can be endangered just as easily by shortages, bad decisions, strange magic, and internal politics as by armies.

The early years brought the Beast of Thumpington, the spreading terror of the Bloom, trouble at Candlemere, and the growing realization that the Stolen Lands contained older powers whose interests did not end when a monster was slain. The Tubthumpers nevertheless survived long enough for a vulnerable frontier project to become a functioning republic.

Varnhold and Vordakai

When Varnhold emptied without explanation, the Tubthumpers followed the trail east into the Valley of the Dead. There they uncovered the work of Vordakai, an ancient cyclops lich who had enslaved the missing settlement. His defeat returned Varnhold’s people and brought the eastern territories into Thumping Waters.

That victory also changed the Republic’s position in the Stolen Lands. Thumping Waters was no longer simply one young realm among many. It had become large enough, wealthy enough, and militarily capable enough that neighboring rulers had to account for it.

Drelev, the Tiger Lords, and Armag

Conflict next drew the Republic toward Fort Drelev, where Baron Hannis Drelev’s failures and alliances had left his people trapped between political betrayal, war, and the dangers of Hooktongue Slough. The Tubthumpers broke Drelev’s rule and expanded the Republic westward.

The struggle then led into war with the Tiger Lords and the rise of Armag the Twice-Born. The Tubthumpers survived the conflict, reached Armag’s Tomb, and ended another attempt to unite the western barbarians against them. By then Thumping Waters had become one of the dominant powers of the Stolen Lands.

The War of the River Kings

King Irovetti of Pitax answered that rise with diplomacy, spectacle, sabotage, and finally war. His invitation to the Rushlight Tournament concealed preparations for a wider conflict. Pitax attacked the Republic with mercenaries, giants, mammoth riders, wyverns, and allied forces, while Thumping Waters fought to keep its settlements intact and dismantle Irovetti’s support.

The war ended inside Pitax itself. The Tubthumpers helped turn discontent into open revolt, entered the city, fought through the House of One Hundred Doors, and killed Irovetti. In the aftermath they recovered the artifact sword Briar and found stronger evidence that the nymph Nyrissa had influenced many of the enemies and disasters that had shaped the Stolen Lands.

Victory left Pitax defeated but did not answer the larger question of why so many threats had converged upon the region—or what Nyrissa ultimately wanted.

After Irovetti

The months following the war brought changes that were less dramatic but no less important. Mairi returned from a private journey involving Amiri’s sword, having cleansed the spirit bound within it, and retired from public adventuring. Leroy gave one final performance before his death. Seamus and Linzi were married in Thumpington.

The Republic also discovered that Seamus’s long-missing father, Mikhail, was still alive. His rescue exposed the truth behind one of Seamus’s oldest enemies: there had never been a human mastermind named Reyoknar. The name was a disguise used by Ilthuliak, an ancient umbral dragon who remains at large.

Exploration resumed in the Glenebon Uplands. The Tubthumpers sighted Minognos-Ushad, the legendary Eater of Kings, and defeated remnants of Kob Moleg’s army. Fitzroy later resigned as Minister of Defense and returned to Brevoy, with Ekundayo succeeding him.

At the same time, signs of growing fey activity spread across the Stolen Lands. Reports of the Wild Hunt added urgency to questions the Republic had postponed during the war: Candlemere, Nyrissa, Briar, and the deeper history of the region.

Beneath Candlemere

On Abadius 18, 4716 AR, the Tubthumpers returned to Candlemere and deliberately broke the ancient Pharasmian spiral seal beneath the ruined tower. The rite opened a shaft into a vast underground complex whose guardians had apparently remained in place for thousands of years. The expedition destroyed seven graveknights, encountered a star-filled void, and learned that the seal was connected to an old cult called the Thresholders.

An undead warrior named Korog claimed that the prison held his brother Foras and that the Thresholders had pursued rites involving Yog-Sothoth, gates, and passages between worlds. Three ancient Morrigna psychopomps later confirmed enough of his account to make the warning impossible to dismiss. They granted the expedition temporary sanctuary and directed the Tubthumpers toward the Hall of the Reliquaries.

The danger was not limited to the ruins. The Oculus of Abaddon, carried by Ant, compelled him to try to push Elowyn into the star-filled void. Elowyn resisted, but the incident proved that the artifact’s influence had escalated from cruelty and violence to an attempt at the direct murder of a companion.

Hunger Wakes

The expedition continued deeper on Abadius 21. They identified surviving Thresholders in chambers that appeared to have been preserved outside ordinary time, fought a banshee later identified as Kyetriel, and explored a scriptorium containing dangerous magical texts and residues. Their recovered notes describe an inverse Spiral of Yog-Sothoth and a ninefold Helix Theorem intended to close a symbolic gate from its center outward. The name Foras appears repeatedly in the surviving record. Session 62 records the expedition’s discoveries in detail.

In a fungus-lit cavern, the party encountered a shoggoth that spoke into their minds with little more than hunger and the cry “Tekeli-li.” It engulfed Seamus and Matteo before Ally destroyed it with force magic. Elsewhere, the Tubthumpers faced Threshold mystics capable of postponing the consequences of mortal wounds, passed through a hall of mirrors that reflected attended objects but not unattended ones, and reached another occupied bunk room whose surviving disciples rose together shouting in Hallit.

The descent eventually went badly enough that the expedition was forced to withdraw. Matteo Auditore and his animal companion Cinder were separated from the others during the retreat, leaving five surviving members to reach the temporary sanctuary maintained by the Morrignas. Rather than abandon them, Ally and Ant sought out former companion Velka and returned to Candlemere with her assistance.

Death Beneath Candlemere

On Abadius 22, the reinforced expedition descended again. The lower complex had not remained quiet: bodies had been removed, additional Thresholder defenders occupied the passages, and the party encountered Mastiffs of Tindalos among creatures associated with disturbances in time. Magical investigation provided evidence that Matteo had survived the original separation, and the Tubthumpers pushed deeper in an effort to reach him.

The renewed advance cost the Republic one of its founding adventurers. Antson “Ant” Crazyfoot was struck down during fighting with the Thresholders, and repeated attempts by his companions to restore him failed. The surviving expedition continued the battle and eventually killed Foras, ending the threat posed by one of the most important Thresholder leaders yet encountered beneath the island.

The expedition found Matteo alive but bound to a worktable, where the Thresholders had subjected him to intrusive memories that made less than a day of captivity feel like countless other lives filled with suffering. With Matteo rescued but Cinder still missing, the party withdrew with Ant’s body rather than risk losing anyone else before they could restore him.

Back in Thumpington, recovered treasure paid for the rare materials required for Tristian to perform Raise Dead. Ant returned to life, weakened but coherent, and bearing a new flame-shaped mark upon his arm. After sufficient recovery, the Tubthumpers went back beneath Candlemere to finish what they had started and bring Cinder home.

No More Curves

The final expedition encountered surviving traps, another Thresholder disciple, and a Leng envoy named Thul Vexir, who had been negotiating with the cult over possible assistance in bringing Yog-Sothoth into Golarion. Vexir showed no loyalty to the Thresholders and little moral objection to their goal. After an exchange of gifts with Elowyn, he ended a supernatural freezing hazard that the party had been unable to solve. The rubies he distributed proved dangerous in their own right: willingly accepting one allows denizens of Leng to direct hostile mental magic against the recipient, and the stones return to those who try to give them away.

Cinder was eventually found alive in a nightmare state guarded by three Quelaunts. He recovered, but his captivity changed him; he can now sense emotional states by scent and answer threats with an emotional effect of his own. His reunion with Ant also awakened a new property in Skybolt, allowing Ant to fly while wielding the bow.

On Abadius 23, Ally completed the ninefold Helix Theorem. The curved passages beneath Candlemere physically straightened, the symbolic gate to Yog-Sothoth was broken, and the oppressive atmosphere that had haunted the island ended. The surviving Thresholder presence was eliminated. Candlemere remains closed while the Republic studies what was left behind, but the ancient mechanism beneath the island is no longer active.

The expedition then spent three months recovering, studying what it had brought back, and adjusting to what Candlemere had done to them. Matteo rebuilt much of his professional training around alchemy, while Ant, Cinder, and Skybolt continued to show lasting changes from death, captivity, and awakened magic.

Toward Numeria

By Gozran, the Republic had begun looking outward again. On Gozran 23, a survey toward a possible trade route crossed into Numeria and encountered two hostile technological constructs that attacked after warning the party that movement was prohibited. The constructs were destroyed, but the encounter made clear that any western road will require more than ordinary frontier surveying.

The end of the Candlemere crisis has therefore returned several older dangers to the foreground. Ilthuliak remains at large. Minognos-Ushad and other threats in the western territories remain unresolved. The Wild Hunt has been sighted, Nyrissa’s influence still hangs over the history of the Stolen Lands, and Briar’s place in that story is not yet understood. The Tubthumpers have survived another ancient catastrophe, but the questions waiting beyond Candlemere have not become smaller while they were underground.
