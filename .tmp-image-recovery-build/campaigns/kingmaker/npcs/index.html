NPCs

The Stolen Lands have never been empty. The Republic of Thumping Waters grew among settlers, rival rulers, neighboring peoples, wandering adventurers, old enemies, fey powers, and citizens who became part of the country long after the Tubthumpers first crossed the frontier.

This archive is organized from the npc_role, npc_affiliations, and npc_status metadata carried by the individual records. Some figures appear in more than one section where their roles overlap; those overlaps are part of the history rather than an error in the index.
