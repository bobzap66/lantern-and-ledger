Bramm

Dwarven Smith and Survivor of Yua's Hope

active

Bramm was a dwarven smith turned fighter and one of the six central survivors of Yua’s Hope in Gravelands Survivors. Born in Yua’s Hope, he spent most of his life around heat, soot, and steel.

Cast-Iron Creations

As a teenager, Bramm began doing odd jobs at Cast-Iron Creations for Rokvel Hardbrow, hauling coal, sorting scrap, and cleaning tools. Rokvel had not been looking for an apprentice, but Bramm kept showing up, worked hard, and never complained when he was given the worst jobs. Eventually, Rokvel began teaching him the smith’s trade properly: repairing armor, maintaining weapons, and reinforcing equipment for the crusaders and travelers who passed through town.

Bramm learned more than metalworking at the forge. Knights and mercenaries often sparred behind the shop while waiting for repairs, and over the years he picked up the basics of fighting by watching them and taking more than a few bruises himself. He never thought of himself as a warrior. He was a smith first.

Six Years Underground

That changed when the undead came.

After taking shelter beneath Yua’s Hope, Bramm spent six years helping Rokvel keep the survivors’ tools, armor, and equipment from falling apart. His instinct remained the same one the forge had taught him: if something was broken, fix it. But as supplies dwindled and hope faded, he increasingly struggled with remaining behind while others risked themselves for the community. Over time, he stopped seeing the hammer as only a tool.

Constan’s death changed the survivors’ circumstances completely. Without him, their food supply could no longer be sustained, and remaining beneath Yua’s Hope was no longer an option. Everyone had to leave the crypt and face whatever remained above.

For Bramm, that forced departure was also the moment when years of preparation became necessary. He did not think of himself as a hero, but after six years underground he was no longer willing to stand back and wait for someone else to fix things. On the journey from Yua’s Hope to Vellumis, he became one of the group’s dependable front-line protectors.

After reaching safety, Bramm chose not to retire from the struggle. Alongside Tomas and Throgg, he offered his service to the Knights of Lastwall and continued the fight against the undead forces that had destroyed the life he once knew.
