Six Were Sent into the Gravelands

Six Were Sent into the Gravelands

Properties1
session recordsGirl Power Versus Death

Sep 23, 20269 min read

The Lantern and Ledger

Six veteran allies of the Knights of Lastwall left Vellumis on Gozran 9 to learn why undead were searching abandoned Knights of Ozem positions around Fort Corlach. At the first outlying site, they found evidence that the search had begun before they arrived—and two creatures that had apparently come afterward.

By Tavian Rusk, Gravelands Correspondent
Events: Gozran 9, 4725 AR
Written: Gozran 11, 4725 AR
Published: Desnus 5, 4725 AR

Contributors

Tavian Rusk War Correspondent

VELLUMIS — Beirivelle Starshine and Aylunna Varvatos summoned six experienced allies of the Knights of Lastwall to Castle Varitan on Gozran 9 with an assignment that was considerably less precise than the people receiving it might have preferred. Undead had been seen searching abandoned holdings of the Knights of Ozem around Fort Corlach. The Knights did not know what they were looking for. Fort Corlach was the strongest candidate for their eventual destination, but several smaller Ozem sites lay around it, and an enemy searching methodically would have reason to examine those as well. Goldie Dawnstar, Jwayne Dawnson, Serafina, Ren Swifttail, Ray Lightbrand and Sebastian Cuthbert Farrington IV were asked to find out what the undead wanted and, if possible, reach it first.

The details of that operation were withheld while the mission remained active. This account was prepared afterward from interviews with the six participants in Vellumis, supplemented where possible by the Knights’ own records. That distinction matters. I did not accompany them into the Gravelands, and combat has never improved anyone’s ability to remember six other people’s positions at once. Their accounts agree on the important sequence and disagree occasionally on the smaller particulars. They also reveal something the official description of a six-person mission does not: this particular group had to become a unit while already carrying out the assignment.

Some of them knew one another well enough to make that process easier, or at least louder. Dawnstar greeted Dawnson by picking him up. She asked after his son before the conversation returned to business. Serafina’s arrival produced a greeting from Dawnson that required considerable editing before it could appear in this newspaper; the publishable portion is, “What are you supposed to be?” Serafina introduced herself politely. Dawnstar subsequently explained to her that Dawnson was “kind of a sourpuss” but actually a nice man. Dawnson did not provide supporting testimony.

Goldie Dawnstar greets Jwayne Dawnson by lifting him from the floor as Serafina arrives at Castle Varitan. Tavian Rusk records the exchange from the edge of the briefing room.

Dawnson knew Swifttail by reputation and Lightbrand from previous service. He expressed surprise that Lightbrand, whose habit of entering dangerous places to keep other people alive is well established, had not yet succeeded in getting himself killed. “Well, perhaps one day,” Lightbrand answered, “but I still serve a purpose for the time being.” Farrington arrived with his own prior acquaintance with Dawnson and a professional concern for the less glamorous requirement of keeping the Knights funded. By the time Starshine and Varvatos began explaining the assignment, the six were not strangers. They were also not a practiced field team, which is a different problem.

The first decision they made was not to go directly to Fort Corlach. The fort remained the main objective, but the outlying Ozem positions offered a chance to learn what the enemy was doing before confronting whatever force might have gathered at the largest site. Farrington argued for gathering those pieces first. Dawnson likewise judged that the stronger fortifications at Corlach might delay an enemy longer than the smaller holdings would. Dawnstar listened to the discussion over the map and announced, “I occur.” Dawnson suggested someone find her crayons. This appears to have concluded the strategic discussion.

Dawnstar also made coffee before the group departed. By her account, there was no operational reason for this beyond six people being present and coffee being available. The magic she used in its preparation happened to allow anyone drinking it to breathe underwater for eight hours. Nothing at their first destination required them to do so. The spell that mattered immediately belonged to Farrington, whose Umbral Journey took the group partly through the Netherworld, where distance is distorted enough to turn days of ordinary travel into hours. The landmarks there are imprecise, so the spell could bring them only within roughly a mile of their intended destination, but within a couple of hours of leaving Vellumis the six were making their final approach to the first outlying site: an abandoned watchtower near the river.

What they found inside was less ambiguous than the briefing. The bones of the old garrison remained on the lower floor among rusted weapons and armor, while the structure itself had survived well enough to suggest that whatever killed those defenders had not needed to batter its way through the walls. Two banshees occupying the tower provided one possible explanation. Their wails badly drained both Dawnstar and Dawnson during the fighting, and the spirits’ ability to remain above much of the group made reaching them a practical problem. Farrington brought one down where the others could engage it and used magic to keep the group fighting effectively; Serafina found ways through the creatures’ defenses; Dawnstar took to the air with her falchion; and Lightbrand and Swifttail attacked where openings allowed. Farrington eventually answered one of the banshees with a ninth-rank chain lightning spell, though not before telling it, “I really must insist you return to the Boneyard.” The spirit did.

Sebastian Cuthbert Farrington IV strikes a banshee with chain lightning as Goldie Dawnstar closes from above. Artist’s reconstruction based on participant interviews.

Dawnstar remembers one of her own attacks rather clearly. She flew up to a banshee, struck it with enough force that the others also remembered the blow, and shouted “Girl power!” There is some ambiguity in the record here. Dawnstar is a champion of Falayna, goddess of femininity, carries a falchion named Girl Power!, and also uses the same words as a battle cry. I attempted during our interview to establish which of the three she meant on this occasion. The distinction did not survive the interview.

Once the banshees were destroyed, the six searched rather than immediately moving on. They recovered enchanted full plate, a shadow rune and an unusually durable shield, useful findings but no obvious answer to the question that had brought them there. Farrington obtained more relevant information through Retrocognition. His examination of the tower’s recent past showed a small group of undead passing through several days earlier and continuing onward. A substantially more dangerous presence arrived later and took up position above the tower. Whatever the first group had been doing there, the occupation of the roof appeared to be a separate event, and Farrington warned the others that what waited above was large and airborne.

It was also not alone. A lesser death armed with a scythe attacked as the group reached the roof, while a gargantuan wyrmwraith fought from the air beyond it. The lesser death made itself the immediate problem by appearing wherever it could most effectively interfere, knocking Dawnson down early in the engagement and disrupting attempts to concentrate on the larger creature. Dawnstar’s description of Dawnson’s reaction was “He big mad.” The tactical version is that he got back on his feet, pursued the lesser death and eventually destroyed it with two blows from his falcata. Both accounts describe substantially the same event.

The wyrmwraith was harder to pin down. It moved rapidly through the air, projected supernatural fear, attacked with void energy and could interfere with magic directed against it. Swifttail found that apparent distance offered little protection when the creature abruptly repositioned beside him, while some of Lightbrand’s holy magic met defenses specifically capable of frustrating it. Dawnstar and Serafina pursued the creature into the air, Farrington worked to keep the others functioning despite its fear, and Dawnson’s destruction of the lesser death finally removed the second enemy that had been dividing their attention. The wyrmwraith still had enough strength to make the closing moments dangerous. When it turned its attacks on Serafina, Dawnstar intercepted one of them rather than let it land on her.

Goldie Dawnstar intercepts the wyrmwraith’s void attack before it can strike Serafina above the ruined watchtower. Artist’s reconstruction based on participant interviews.

Dawnstar also struck the wyrmwraith with Girl Power! while shouting “Girl power!” This does not resolve the earlier ambiguity.

The six eventually destroyed the wyrmwraith, but killing it explained very little. Serafina’s examination indicated that the creature possessed a relationship with sunlight that should have made its presence on the exposed roof more difficult than it plainly was. The group had no explanation for that discrepancy when I interviewed them. Nor could they establish that the wyrmwraith had anything to do with the smaller undead group Farrington had seen passing through the tower earlier. Sequence is not allegiance. What their evidence did establish was that undead had searched this old Knights of Ozem position before the six arrived and then moved on.

That was enough to justify the decision to examine the outlying sites before Fort Corlach. The first tower did not reveal what the enemy wanted, but it showed that the search described by Starshine and Varvatos was real, organized and already in progress. It also left the six with several pieces of information they had not possessed in Vellumis: a prior undead party had visited the tower, its interest did not appear to have ended there, and the object of the search was not obvious among the valuables left behind. They had reached the first site within hours of accepting the mission. Fort Corlach was still ahead of them.
